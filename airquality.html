Health Advisories & recommendations

Wear a Mask Outdoors

Sensitive groups are advised to wear a particulate respirator mask (N95 or equivalent) outdoors today.

Close Windows and Doors

Keep windows and doors closed to avoid clean indoor air mixing with unhealthy outdoor atmospheric particulates.

Reduce Outdoor Activities

Try to reduce heavy or prolonged exercise outdoors, particularly in high-traffic or heavily populated sectors.

Use Air Purifiers

If available, run indoor air purifiers on automated speed modes to keep particulate levels low inside your residence.
