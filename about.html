Our Mission

GreenGuard is a state-of-the-art smart environmental monitoring system designed to build clean, healthy, and resilient ecosystems in modern cities. We empower local citizens and municipal administrations with real-time tracking of fine atmospheric particulates, environmental alert forecasts, and gamified eco-friendly checklists to encourage collective action towards building a healthier tomorrow.

Accuracy

Delivering precise climate data gathered from IoT sensor registries.

Community

Encouraging community action programs for clean environment maintenance.
