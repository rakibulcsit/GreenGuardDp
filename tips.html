Daily Environmental Tips

Conserve Household Water

Shorten your daily shower time by just 2 minutes or turn off the faucet when brushing teeth to save over 100 gallons of clean water per week.

Water

Eliminate Phantom Power Draw

Unplug phone chargers, microwaves, and standby television boxes when heading out. They continue drawing power even when inactive, contributing to your carbon footprints.

Energy

Transition to Reusables

Avoid single-use paper cups, plastic grocery bags, and plastic water bottles. Keeping a cloth canvas bag and reusable flasks drastically minimizes municipal landfill waste volume.

Waste

Green Transportation Practices

Try to walk, ride a bicycle, or utilize public metro transits for short commutes. This decreases individual carbon emissions by nearly 85% compared to private fuel vehicles.
